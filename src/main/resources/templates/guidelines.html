Blood Donation Guidelines

Everything a donor should know before donating blood.

Blood Donation Instructions
  • Eat a healthy meal and avoid fatty food before donating.
  • Drink plenty of water in the hours before your donation.
  • Carry a valid photo ID and your Registration ID to the camp.
  • Get a good night's sleep before the donation day.
  • Wear clothing with sleeves that can be raised above the elbow.
  • Inform the staff about any medication you are currently taking.
  • After donating, rest for 10–15 minutes and have the refreshments provided.
  • Avoid heavy lifting or strenuous exercise for the rest of the day.
Donor Eligibility Guidelines
  • You must be between 18 and 65 years of age.
  • You should weigh at least 50 kg.
  • Your haemoglobin level should be at least 12.5 g/dL.
  • A gap of at least 3 months is required between two donations.
  • You should be free from cold, fever or infection on donation day.
  • You should not have donated blood in the last 90 days.
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women should not donate.
  • You must not be under the influence of alcohol in the last 24 hours.
Final eligibility is always confirmed by medical staff at the camp.
