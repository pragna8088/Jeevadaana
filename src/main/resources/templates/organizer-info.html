Organizer Instructions

How to organize and manage a blood donation camp on Jeevadaana.

Getting Started
  1. Register your organization to receive a unique Organizer ID.
  2. Log in and open your Organizer Dashboard.
  3. Click Organize New Camp and fill in the camp details (date, time, venue, district, capacity).
  4. Publish the camp so donors can find and register for it.
Managing & Post-Camp
  1. Use Manage to view registered donors for each camp.
  2. On the camp day, record each donor's blood units collected.
  3. Review post-camp statistics: total registrations, total donors, units collected and a blood-group-wise breakdown.
  4. Mark the camp Completed once donations are recorded.
  5. You can edit upcoming camps or delete camps with no donations.
Ensure trained medical staff and proper storage are arranged for every camp.
